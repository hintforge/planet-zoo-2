## Research trees [ref 4 (wikily.gg/planet-zoo-2/research)]
| Tree | Topics | Levels | What each level does |
|---|---|---|---|
| Species Research | 136 | 3 to 6 per species | reveals a thriving requirement; raises fertility by 15%, then 30%; adds the species fact sheet as an education topic; enables its release groups |
| Disease Research | 52 | 3 | raises immunity of every species that can catch the disease by 25% / 50% / 75% and lowers its lethality |
| Enrichment Research | 22 | 1 | each topic unlocks a set of toys or feeders (84 enrichment items in total) |
| Education Research | 7 | 1 | adds education topics or stations (37 topics, 8 stations: education boards, conservation boards, speakers, interactive stations) |

## Conservation projects [ref 8 (wikily.gg/planet-zoo-2/conservation-projects)]
- 6 project types: Animal Reintroduction, Research, Habitat Restoration, Habitat Connectivity, Outreach, Wildlife Care. At capture: 10 weekly projects and 2 Career projects (e.g. "Binturong Behaviour"; weekly examples "A Better Life" (Wildlife Care, Thailand), "A Grey Area", "Aardvark Reintroduction", "Arti-fish-al", "Black-Eyed Plea", "Dive Safely", "Man, oh, Mangrove", "Reef Relief", "Relocation, Relocation, Relocation", "Scute Along Now", "Within a Whisker").
  _source: P1 result 2026-10-19 [ref 8 (wikily.gg/planet-zoo-2/conservation-projects)] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ `[Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]`
- Projects cost cash, staff time, animals and release groups; staff sent on a project leave the zoo for a while. Rewards are mostly Conservation Credits; some give animals or release groups, claimed under **Gifts in Animal Trading**. Every project is offered by FEN.
  _source: P1 result 2026-10-19 [ref 8 (wikily.gg/planet-zoo-2/conservation-projects)] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: medium · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ `[Single source -- verify · class:community-wiki]`
- Weekly projects rotate, so a missed one may not return; whether it does is unknown. Achievement "Ultimate Conservationist" needs one project of every type; weekly rotation may limit availability (see `../achievements.md`).
  _source: P1 result 2026-10-19 [ref 8 (wikily.gg/planet-zoo-2/conservation-projects)] · capture: browser_fetch · confidence: low · enemy-tier: 0 · puzzle-tier: 0 · category: mainline · spoiler: none_ `[Hypothesis -- unverified]`
